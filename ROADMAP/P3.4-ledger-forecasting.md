# P3.4 — Ledger forecasting

**Issue:** [#164](https://github.com/Amitcoh1/agentbreaker/issues/164)

**Vision:** VISION.md §7 · Phase 3 · **P3.4**. Forecasting on the ledger.

## Scope
- **Trajectory-based spend projection** (where is this run/tenant heading given its burn shape).
- **What-if** on routing/policy changes (project the cost impact before deploying).

## Definition of done
- [ ] A run/tenant's projected end-of-period spend is computed from its trajectory.
- [ ] A what-if compares projected spend under two policy/routing configs.
- [ ] Projections are surfaced in the FinOps views (P2.4).

## Phase gate
Phase 3 (+10 → +18mo). Depends on a populated event-sourced ledger (P2.1) with enough history to project.
