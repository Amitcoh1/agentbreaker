## Scope (thinnest slice)

1. **Guide — "agents can't predict their own spend"** (EN + HE). Anchored in the Stanford Digital
   Economy Lab findings (VISION §1): ~1000x tokens vs chat, up to 30x cost variance on identical tasks,

   forecast a fat tail, you can only bound it.*
2. **Demo video** — a runaway loop getting tripped. Reuse `examples/runaway_demo` ($12.63 → $0.82).
   Show the trip + the explanation object.
3. **Comparison page** — honest, names **AgentBudget / Alephant / LiteLLM** with their real gaps from
   VISION §4 (the "no player has more than one of the five" framing). Lands on marketing.
